Privacy First

🔒 Our Commitment

At Spino, we believe your privacy is fundamental. We've designed our app with a simple philosophy: your data stays on your device.

✓ No account required — Use Spino without signing up
✓ No data collection — We don't collect any personal information
✓ No tracking — No analytics, no cookies, no monitoring
✓ Offline first — Works completely without internet

📊 Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

Spino operates entirely on your device. We have no servers that receive your data, no accounts to manage, and no way to access what you create in the app.

💾 Local Data Storage

Everything you create in Spino is stored locally on your device:

  • Decision Lists — Your custom wheels and options
  • Spin History — Records of your spin results
  • App Preferences — Your settings and customizations

Your control: You can delete individual items or all your data at any time directly from the app. When you uninstall Spino, all locally stored data is automatically removed from your device.

⚙️ App Permissions

Spino requests minimal permissions:

  • Vibration — Used for haptic feedback when spinning the wheel, providing a more engaging experience

We do not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or any other sensitive device features.

🔗 Third-Party Services

Spino does not integrate with any third-party services that would collect your data. We don't use:

  • Analytics platforms
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media integrations
  • Cloud storage services

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Children's Privacy

Spino is safe for users of all ages. Since we don't collect any personal information, there are no special concerns regarding children's privacy. The app contains no inappropriate content and no in-app purchases.

🛡️ Data Security

Your data is protected by your device's built-in security features. Since all information stays on your device and is never transmitted, the security of your data depends on your device's security settings (screen lock, encryption, etc.).

📝 Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
